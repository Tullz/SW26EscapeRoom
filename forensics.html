Case file: The Friday Night Leak

On Saturday morning, the secret designs for Project Aurora, next year's big game from Copperleaf Games, turned up on a rival studio's website. Someone took them from the office on Friday night.

Rosa Hollis, the head of the studio, has called your team in. The board meets at 9 and she needs to know exactly what happened. Glen, the night guard, has pinned everything he could find to the evidence board.

  1. Rebuild Friday night. Search the board. Every moment you uncover becomes a card. Put the cards in the order they happened.
  2. Name the culprit. Work out who was behind it.
  3. Prove it. Pin the right evidence to their photo and close the case.
Just for fun

There are no points to win or lose in this bonus room. Hints are free and wrong guesses cost nothing, so your team's score stays exactly as it is.
