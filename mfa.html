Mission briefing: Priya's account

Priya Shah, a finance manager at Northwind, typed her work password into a fake login page this morning. She's been phished, and an attacker now has her password. Priya is stuck on a train across town, and her work phone is locked in her desk drawer.

You're Northwind's IT security helpers. Priya has asked you to go to her desk, sign in to her account with her permission, and help Sam from IT secure it before the attacker gets in. She's on a call with you and will guide you.

  1. Search Priya's desk. Click anything that looks interesting. Useful clues are saved in your notebook.
  2. Sign in to her account using the clues she left, so Sam can lock it down.
  3. Guard her phone. The attacker will try to trick whoever is holding it. Spot the tricks and refuse them.
