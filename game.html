    How scoring works
    • Points kept: your team starts with 100 points. Using a hint or giving a wrong answer costs a few, so think before you click.
    • Rooms: you get 20 points for every room you solve.
    • Vault: you get 50 points when you crack it.
    • Time bonus: when your facilitator runs the 60-minute clock, every whole minute still left when you crack the vault is worth 2 points. For example, crack it with 20 minutes to spare and you get 40 extra points.
    • When time runs out, the rooms and the vault close. Only what you finished in time counts.
    • Bonus room: just for fun. It doesn't change your score.
    • Winning: the highest total wins. If two teams have the same total, the team that cracked the vault first wins.

    Stuck on a room? Use its hint button, or ask a facilitator.
