How the game works

Your team works through five security escape rooms: Phishing, OSINT, Incident Management, MFA and Encryption. Every room you solve gives you a code word. Put all five words into the vault on the Mission Hub to crack it.

  • Log in with your team name and password. Your progress is shared, so you can split up across laptops.
  • Play the rooms in any order. The Mission Hub shows your next step and your score.
  • You start with 100 points. Hints and wrong answers cost a few; solving rooms and cracking the vault earn more. The sooner you crack the vault, the bigger your time bonus.
  • Finished early? Cracking the vault unlocks a bonus room, just for fun.
