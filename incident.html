The Midnight Alert

It's 2:13 AM. A security alarm has just gone off at Meridian and your team is on call. Find out what's happening — and deal with it.

Your mission

When something bad happens, good teams do four things. You'll do them in order, one step at a time. Every step has clues on screen — no technical knowledge needed.

  1. Spot it — work out who is really behind the alarm.
  2. Stop it — stop the damage from getting worse.
  3. Tell — call the right people.
  4. Fix it — find how they got in and close that door.

Wrong guesses cost 2 points. Stuck? Each step has a hint for 5 points.

      Case closed

      You spotted it, stopped it, told the right people and fixed the cause. That's incident response.

      • Spot it: something that doesn't add up — like a log-in from somewhere impossible — is worth reporting straight away.
      • Stop it: act fast to limit the damage: cut off the affected system and lock the account, but keep the evidence.
      • Tell: tell the right people quickly — not everyone, and never publicly first.
      • Fix it: find how they got in and close that door. A different password for every site, plus a phone code at log-in, stops most attacks like this one.

      If you ever notice something odd at work, report it to IT or security straight away — you could be the one who spots it.
