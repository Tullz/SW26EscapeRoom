2:13 AM. An alarm is going off in the Ops Room at Larkspur, and the number on the screen keeps climbing.

Mission briefing: the Ops Room

Ray, Larkspur's night security guard, has called your team in. An alarm says customer records are being copied out of the company, right now. Ray doesn't know computers, so it's over to you.

  1. Search the room. Click anything that looks interesting. Useful finds go into your Evidence list.
  2. Follow the Incident Playbook (its own panel in the room). It has four steps: Spot it, Stop it, Tell, Fix it.
  3. Connect the clues. No technical knowledge needed. Everything you need is somewhere in the room.
