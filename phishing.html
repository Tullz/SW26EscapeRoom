Four emails just landed in John Price's inbox. Some are traps. Find out which, and prove it.

Mission briefing

John from Finance thinks someone is targeting him. Go through his inbox one email at a time, like a security analyst would.

  1. Inspect the email. Click or tap anything that looks wrong to drop a red flag on it.
  2. Decide whether the email is phishing or legitimate.
  3. Submit. Only then will you find out whether you were right, and see what you missed.

Points intel

Teams begin at 100 points. Careful work keeps that total high.

  • Wrong submission (wrong verdict, or not enough warning signs found): −3 points, then try again.
  • Hints get more specific each time: −5, −5, then a spotlight on one warning sign for −10.
  • You can drop up to 6 flags per email. Flags on harmless parts of the email count against you, so don't carpet-bomb it.

          Phishing Spotter — How It Works

          Four emails have landed in John's inbox. Some are phishing, some might be genuine. Your job is to find out which, and prove it.

          1. Inspect an email and click or tap each suspicious detail to drop a red flag. Click a flag to remove it.
          2. Pick your verdict: Phishing or Legitimate.
          3. Press Submit highlights. You'll only find out if you were right after you submit. Wrong answers cost 3 points.
          4. Hints are available but cost points.
          5. Clear all four emails to unlock this room's code word.
