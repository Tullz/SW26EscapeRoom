It's early Monday morning and the security team's phones are all ringing at once. Over the weekend, someone discovered that customer data has been quietly leaking for weeks.

The source? A routine software update from one of Nexus Corp's trusted vendors — but someone had hidden malicious code inside it. It's like a delivery driver with a trusted uniform sneaking something dangerous into your building.

Your team has been called in to investigate. Trace how the attackers got in, learn from real-world cases, and choose the right defenses to prevent it from happening again.
