Mission briefing: the courier's case

Last night a criminal crew stole Harbourline Insurance's customer records, and tonight they plan to sell them. Police stopped one of the crew's couriers at Central Station. He isn't talking, and everything in his briefcase is written in code.

Detective Sergeant Kemi Adeyemi has asked Harbourline's security team (that's you) to help. The courier is in the interview room next door, and the microphone is on.

  1. Search the briefcase. Click anything to look at it up close, and turn things over. Useful finds go in your notebook.
  2. Crack the crew's codes. Every code has a key, and the keys are hidden somewhere in the case.
  3. Find the handover password before the buyer turns up.
